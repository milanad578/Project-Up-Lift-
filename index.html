📖 Common Student Scams & What To Watch For

💳

Upfront Fees & "Equipment Checks"

They send a counterfeit check and ask you to buy laptops or send gift cards to their "supplier". The bank later claws back the bad check, leaving you liable.

⏳

Manufactured Urgency

"Act within 24 hours or lose your spot!" Pressure tactics are designed to bypass your logical caution before you can consult an advisor.

⭐

"Guaranteed" Scholarships

Real scholarship committees never guarantee awards before rigorous application reviews, nor do they charge "disbursement fees".

🔒

Immediate Sensitive Data Requests

Asking for your Social Security Number, passport copy, or banking credentials before a formal contract or secure onboarding platform.
